# Interview guide and notes template

Use this for every interview. Do not write findings here until the interview has actually happened.
Keep personal details out of the report: use P1, P2, W1 (warden), A1 (admin) as codes.

## Before you start
- Read the consent statement aloud and get verbal or written agreement.
- Say that they can skip any question or stop at any time.
- Ask permission before taking notes or recording.
- Aim for 20 to 30 minutes. Interview at least 5 students from different blocks and years, plus every warden and one administrator you can reach.

**Consent statement:** "I am a student doing a project on how hostel complaints are handled. I will ask about your experience. Your name will not appear in the report. You can skip any question or stop whenever you like. Is it okay to continue?"

## Questions for students
1. Tell me about the last time you had a problem in your room or the hostel. What happened?
2. How did you report it? To whom?
3. How long did you wait for a response? Did you hear back?
4. Did you ever decide not to report something? Why?
5. How did you know whether anyone was working on it?
6. What would make you trust the process more?
7. Is there anything you would be uncomfortable reporting in person?
8. What phone or computer do you usually use?

## Questions for wardens
1. Walk me through what happens when a complaint arrives.
2. How do you record complaints today? Where?
3. How many complaints in a typical week? Which kinds are most common?
4. How do you decide what is urgent?
5. How do you tell maintenance staff, and how do you know the work was done?
6. What goes wrong most often in this process?
7. What information do you wish a complaint always included?

## Questions for administrators
1. What do you currently find out about hostel problems, and when?
2. What would you want to see in a monthly summary?
3. What decisions would better information help you make?
4. Who is responsible if a complaint is not handled in time?
